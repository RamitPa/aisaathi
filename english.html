Talk English with Mr. Reed

Practise speaking English out loud on your phone. Free with the Claude app.

1Choose your lesson and copy your teacher

See or copy the text yourself

2Paste it into Claude

  1. Open the Claude app and start a new chat. Sign in or sign up for free if you are asked.
  2. Paste the text and send it.
Open Claude

Don't have the app? Get "Claude by Anthropic" from the App Store or Google Play.

3Start talking

In the same chat, tap the voice button next to the microphone, then say "Hello". Mr. Reed answers out loud and corrects you as you talk.

4Get your report card

When you finish, say "Give me my report". Press and hold Mr. Reed's report message in Claude, copy it, and paste it here.

Press and hold the image to save it or share it on WhatsApp.

Tips

  • Didn't understand? Say "Please say that again slowly."
  • At the end, say "Give me my report." He gives you your scores, mistakes and new words. Then do step 4.
  • Practise again tomorrow in the same chat, so he remembers you.
  • On the free Claude plan you get a limited number of voice messages, so after a while you may need to wait before continuing.
